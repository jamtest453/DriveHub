YOUR VEHICLE CRITERIA

Create a car search

Search websites by car name. Required and excluded terms filter combined adverts in this app; preferred terms rank matches higher without narrowing searches on Facebook or other providers.

Reads supported URL parameters into the fields below. Review the criteria and any unsupported filters before saving. Your original link is not saved.

Use OR or | for alternatives within a group. Commas separate groups. Example: C4S OR Carrera 4S, manual means (C4S or Carrera 4S) and manual.

Optional words or phrases, separated by commas. Matching cars appear first under Best preferred match; other cars remain visible. Existing extra keywords become preferred keywords.

Separate unwanted words or phrases with commas. A match in any grouped advert excludes the car from this search. Matching ignores capitalisation and punctuation. Saving changes updates existing results immediately.

Body type — select any

If no body style is specified in an advert, the vehicle is still included. Only confirmed, unwanted body types are excluded.

Exterior colours — select any

Cars with unconfirmed exterior colour are included by default. Untick to require a confirmed selected colour. Colours of seats and wheels are not treated as body paint.

SAVED CARS

Add a car from a listing link

Paste the URL of an individual advert from Auto Trader, eBay, PistonHeads or Facebook Marketplace. The car is saved immediately and the app tries to fill missing details from the website. You can enter or correct anything yourself.

Manual changes take precedence over future scans and auto-enrichment. Empty fields on a new car can be completed automatically where the source allows access.
FACEBOOK MARKETPLACE

Connect Facebook

Car Search Hub needs to be signed into Facebook in its own browser on the server, not just in the Facebook app on your phone. This checks Marketplace access before claiming it is connected.

Your Facebook login. Used only to sign this server's browser into your Facebook so Marketplace searches run as you. It is kept in your own Google Drive (in Car Search's private app folder), never shown again here, and not included in backups.

Facebook connection has not been checked yet.

Car Search has not confirmed a working Marketplace session. First select View / complete Facebook challenge to see whether it shows a login form, a security check or an error. Facebook may not send any phone notification. Only use Recheck or enter a code if Facebook actually requests it.

Complete Facebook's challenge: Tap a button or input on the screen below. If Facebook asks for text or a code, tap the appropriate field first, then use the controls beneath it. This is Car Search's own browser, live, not your phone's Facebook session.

When the challenge is complete, select Recheck Marketplace access. The app only saves a session after Facebook actually allows Marketplace results. Do not share this screen if it shows personal details.

Any Facebook challenge must be completed by you. The app does not bypass verification or transfer your phone's Facebook login to Car Search.

Changed your Facebook password? Clear the previous server session first, then connect again. This does not remove saved car searches or notes.
