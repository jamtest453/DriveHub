The Road Score estimates how rewarding a road is to drive, from its geometry, flow, scenery and surface quality.

  • Geometry — bends, rhythm and direction change.
  • Flow — enough uninterrupted road to feel worthwhile.
  • Road quality — width, lanes, surface and condition.
  • Scenery — viewpoints, water, woodland and open country.
  • Confidence — how complete the underlying map data is.

Road Score is a discovery aid, not a safety rating. Always follow local signs, access restrictions and conditions.
