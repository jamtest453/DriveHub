RELEASE HISTORY

What's new

v142.32 · 17 November 2026

Price-to-mileage badges and best-value sorting

Car Search listing cards now show Great price, Good price, Fair price, High price, Poor price or Not rated, based on relative asking prices and mileage among comparable current listings. Lower prices and lower mileage improve the rating; comparisons are scoped to the same model/trim and nearby model years.

Added Best price vs mileage to result sorting, using the same scoring. A qualitative badge requires at least three comparable cars with reliable details; results with limited data remain sortable but are not presented as independently verified bargains.

v142.31 · 17 November 2026

Clearer refresh status, better car locations and right-aligned tablet navigation

Removed the redundant progress label beside each search; the Refresh now button still switches to Refreshing while its scan is active.

Location map lookups now use clean advertised town/postcode information without the “multiple locations” conflict label or provider mileage hints. On tablets, the shared DriveHub navigation controls sit against the right edge rather than drifting left.

v142.30 · 17 November 2026

Reliable refreshes, faster pages and editable cars

Removed unreliable photo-colour estimates from results, filters and duplicate decisions. Colours from advert information and manual edits are still supported; image fingerprints remain in use for matching duplicate photos.

Every result can now be edited without saving it to the shortlist. Searches show a dedicated Refreshing indicator and current website while scanning; source timeouts and safe browser cleanup prevent indefinite busy states.

Car Search now caches stable grouping, reduces redundant header/page renders and stops reloading the whole dashboard for every photo fingerprint, improving navigation responsiveness.

v142.29 · 17 November 2026

Manage searches in one place, with distances beside locations

Car Search's header now offers Overview, Searches and Saved cars instead of a tab for every saved search. The new Searches page shows each search's last check, deduplicated cars found and recent updates, with one-click Refresh now and Open results actions.

Car listings now place distance directly after the town in brackets, such as Hayes (157 miles), rather than in a separate specification. Distance is an approximate straight-line figure from High Lane, Stockport, loaded as location information becomes available.

v142.28 · 17 November 2026

Less clutter, larger car photos and one-line navigation

Car Search now folds its source links into a collapsed panel. Result cards show more of the car photo, suppress repeated title and provider details, and keep their existing shortlist, notes, map and duplicate-management actions.

Every tool's navigation now sits alongside the DriveHub title in a single shared header, with one consistent Home shortcut. New searches include unconfirmed colours by default, and non-AI image colour estimates need stronger agreement and at least 86% confidence. Existing preferences and saved listings remain intact.

v142.27 · 17 November 2026

One tool-specific header and smarter make/model searches

Car Search's saved searches, shortlist, new search, Facebook and settings options now live in DriveHub's shared header. On smaller screens, the saved searches are available from a compact selector. Other tools also display their own navigation in the header, and the DriveHub icon returns Home.

Auto Trader and PistonHeads now use the recognised make and model from the search name, falling back to keywords where the model is unknown. Car Search's redundant full-screen link is removed. Previously saved cars and scan history are unchanged.

v142.26 · 16 November 2026

Photo colour estimates, location maps and one DriveHub header

Car Search now estimates paint colour from advert photos without AI, with strict confidence thresholds and manual/advert colours taking priority. Click the location or green map pin on any listing to open an approximate, zoomed-out regional map. Home and all four tools now share the same light DriveHub header; select the logo to return to Home.

v142.25 · 16 November 2026

Car Search now looks and behaves like DriveHub

Fixed the tiny Car Search window seen when opening it from DriveHub: the embedded tool now fills the available browser space on phones and desktops. Updated the original Car Search interface with DriveHub typography, colour palette, logo, navigation, controls and cards, without changing search results or saved cars.

Also corrected the standalone launcher's Car Search link to point to the current DriveHub service.

v142.24 · 16 November 2026

Full original Car Search Hub restored

Restores the complete saved original Car Finder application, including its Playwright browser-based scanner, Facebook session manager, eBay integration, photo comparison, geocoded map, editable saved cars and original interface.

Everything now runs inside the single DriveHub Railway service. The original backend and all its tests are included in the DriveHub repository. The service uses the existing DriveHub volume; a Car Search password protects private saved-car data.

Website scan success still depends on the sites allowing automated access and any required account credentials.

v142.24 · 16 November 2026

Car Search now runs inside DriveHub

Car Search now has its own native backend and user interface in the DriveHub repository. No separate Car Finder service or domain is required.

  • Persistent searches and saved cars now live in DriveHub's existing /data volume, with import/export backups.
  • Add cars from supported advert URLs; edit vehicle details, notes and asking prices; save, share, hide and restore cars.
  • Keyword AND/OR combinations, duplicate grouping and merging, display filters and generated search links.
  • Source refresh attempts read public pages and clearly report site restrictions without inventing adverts.

Scanning limitation: This is a rebuilt Node version. The former Playwright/Facebook authentication, full maps and image hashing are not yet reproduced. Some sites block server-side HTML access; use original links or manual listing entry when that happens.

Privacy: Set CAR_SEARCH_PASSWORD in Railway to protect stored searches and private notes.

v142.22 · 16 November 2026

Car Search joins DriveHub

DriveHub now brings together four tools. Car Search is available beside MyGarage, Valuation Tool and Road Finder, with its own tool page at /search/.

  • Browse and manage your existing Car Search searches, merged results, saved cars and notes from within DriveHub.
  • Use the existing Car Search account and persistent data; the original car-searching service remains independent.
  • DriveHub's server safely forwards Car Search requests through a dedicated path, keeping its session and API separate from other tools.
  • The mobile and standalone DriveHub launchers also display Car Search and open its online service when selected.
  • All DriveHub checks and new Car Search integration tests pass.

Hosting note: Car Search's Python backend must remain running. DriveHub uses the existing Railway endpoint unless configured with CAR_SEARCH_HUB_URL. No saved data is copied or migrated.

v142.21

MyGarage history and navigation improvements

The previous release improved mobile MOT history containment and separated the Timeline into Upcoming, Today and Past events. All existing tools remain available.
